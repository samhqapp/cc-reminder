# CC Reminder Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: May 7, 2026

CC Reminder is a local-first credit card due-date reminder app. It helps you remember card billing and due dates without requiring an account or a backend service.

## Data Stored On Your Device

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Card nickname | Identify the reminder in the app |
| Provider | Organize reminders by issuer or provider |
| Last 4 digits | Distinguish cards without storing a full card number |
| Billing day and due day | Calculate reminder timing |
| Tags and notes | Organize local reminder details |
| Reminder state | Track acknowledged, snoozed, and settled reminders |
| Notification preferences | Decide which reminders should schedule local notifications |

## Data Not Collected

| Data | Status |
|---|---|
| Full card number | Not collected |
| CVV | Not collected |
| Expiry date | Not collected |
| Payment credentials | Not collected |
| Bank login | Not collected |
| Bank connection | Not collected |
| Account credentials | Not collected |

## Sharing And Sale

CC Reminder does not share or sell user data. It has no cloud sync, account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or payment processor integration.

## Crash Reports

If CC Reminder quits unexpectedly, it asks on the next launch whether to send a crash report. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. The report holds the error, the app version, and the platform version, never your cards or notes.

## Daily Counts

Settings has a switch "Share anonymous daily counts", off by default. When you turn it on, the app sends one count a day with the app version, platform, and platform version. It holds no cards, notes, or account. A random install id limits counts to one a day; the server keeps it in memory only and stores daily totals.

## Notifications

CC Reminder uses local notifications on your device. Notification content is generated from locally stored reminder data.

## Deleting Data

Cards and notes can be deleted inside the app. A single clear-all-local-data control was not found in the current source and should be added before public production release.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the support contact configured in Google Play Console.
